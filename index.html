Rating (0–10): calm wind drives it, so near-glassy conditions score 10 and every extra km/h costs points. Gusts, rain and wet sand (rain in the previous 12 hours) scale it down, as do feels-like temperatures below 20°C or above 32°C. Feels-like includes sun, wind and humidity. Buckets only count hours between sunrise and 15 minutes after sunset. Wind from the bay (S–SW, onshore) counts up to 15% stronger; wind from the land (N–NE, offshore) up to 25% weaker, since the beach is sheltered from it.
